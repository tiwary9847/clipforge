# ViralKaro – Privacy policy
_Last updated: [date]_

**Short version: your videos never leave your device.**

- **Editing:** videos, photos and audio are processed on your phone or computer. They are not uploaded to us.
- **Saved on your device:** projects, settings and your licence code are stored in your browser/app storage. Clearing site data removes them.
- **When you buy Pro:** you give your email to Razorpay (website) or Google Play (app) to pay. We receive your email, the payment ID and the plan, and keep them to issue and restore your licence. We never see your card or UPI details.
- **Built-in AI (Pro):** the text you send to the AI (for example your request to the assistant, or caption text for titles and hooks) is sent through our server to Google Gemini to get an answer. For **Comment preview** with AI, up to 5 small still frames of your video are sent too. For **AI voices**, the text to be spoken is sent. We count requests per licence for the monthly limit; we don’t keep the text, frames or audio. If you add your own AI key, requests go straight from your device to that provider.
- **GIF search:** the words you search for and a random ID made on your device (not your name or email) are sent through our server to KLIPY to find GIFs and stickers.
- **YouTube posting (optional):** if you choose to post to YouTube, you sign in with Google and allow ViralKaro to upload videos to your channel. The permission is used only to upload the videos and thumbnails you choose, from your device straight to YouTube. We don’t receive or store your Google account details, sign-in token or YouTube data, and we don’t share them with anyone. You can remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. ViralKaro’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
- **Optional online features** (stock videos, music and sound libraries, memes, maps, AI models) load content from those services when you use them; their privacy policies apply.
- **No ads, no tracking.** We don’t use advertising or analytics trackers.
- **Your rights:** email [email] to get a copy of, or delete, the purchase record linked to your email.
