# Policy pages for Razorpay

Razorpay asks for these pages on your website before it switches on live payments. Fill in every `[…]`, then ask Claude to publish them (they become `/clipforge/legal/…` and get linked from the Pro screen).

- `terms.md` – Terms of use
- `privacy.md` – Privacy policy
- `refunds.md` – Cancellation & refund policy
- `contact.md` – Contact us

These are plain-language starting points, not legal advice. Have them checked if you're unsure.
