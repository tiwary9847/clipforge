# ClipForge – Cancellation, refund & delivery policy
_Last updated: [date]_

- **Delivery.** ClipForge Pro is a digital licence – nothing is shipped. It switches on instantly on your device after payment, and you can restore it any time with your email and payment ID.
- **No auto-renewal.** The 30-day and 12-month passes end on their own; there is nothing to cancel.
- **7-day refund.** If Pro doesn’t work for you, email [email] within 7 days of paying with your payment ID (starts with `pay_`). We refund the full amount to the original payment method within 5–7 working days and switch off that licence.
- **Google Play purchases** are refunded through Google Play’s refund process.
- **Payment taken but Pro not switched on?** Open ClipForge Pro → “Already bought Pro? Get it back”, enter your email and payment ID. If that fails, email us and we’ll fix it within 2 working days.
